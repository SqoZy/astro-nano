Doomed is a doom inspired doomshooter made in Unreal Engine. I worked on Doomed in a team for the past three months.
We started with a base game that was already made and started polishing the game by adding shaders and cinematics. whe also added a boss and changed the level design so the player is forced to learn the basic gameplay before fighting the boss.
