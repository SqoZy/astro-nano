I built this Snake game as a fun way to practice JavaScript and game logic. Inspired by the classic Snake games and tutorials online, I wanted to recreate the experience with my own twist.

The game lets you control the snake with your keyboard, eat food to grow longer, and try to avoid running into yourself or the walls. It's a great exercise in handling user input, collision detection, and simple game loops.

**Twist:**  
There are now two different types of food. One type increases your tail by a small amount, while the other gives a much bigger tail increase.
