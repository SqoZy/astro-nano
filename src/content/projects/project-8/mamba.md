Building on the foundation of my Snake game, I created Mamba as an enhanced version with additional features and improvements. This game takes the classic Snake mechanics and adds my own creative twist to make it more engaging.

The core gameplay remains familiar - control your mamba, eat food to grow longer, and avoid colliding with yourself or the walls. However, I've added new elements to make the experience more interesting and challenging.

This project helped me practice iterative game development, taking a working concept and expanding upon it with new features and polish.
